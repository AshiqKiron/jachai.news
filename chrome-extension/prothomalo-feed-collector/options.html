What to collect

Prothom Alo exposes top-level sections as /api/v1/collections/{name}.rss. Nested pages like bangladesh/capital usually share the parent section feed — there is no separate RSS for every sub-page.

More RSS URLs

Add any RSS/Atom feed URL (e.g. other newspapers). Give each a short label — they appear in the list above so you can enable/disable them like section feeds.

Automatic schedule

Chrome’s timer allows at least 1 minute between runs (values smaller than 1 are treated as 1 minute).
